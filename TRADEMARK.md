# Trademark Policy

The code in this repository is MIT-licensed (see `LICENSE`). The **Unpeel
name, logo, app icon, and mascot are not** — they identify software built and
operated by UX Themes AS, and this policy is what keeps a fork from being
mistaken for the real thing.

## You can, without asking

- Use "Unpeel" to truthfully refer to this project: articles, talks,
  tutorials, package descriptions ("a client for Unpeel", "works with
  Unpeel").
- Build the software from this source for yourself, your team, or your own
  machines, keeping the name — private builds are not distribution.
- Ship integrations, agents, themes, or Unpeel Apps that state compatibility
  ("for Unpeel"), as long as the branding makes clear who made what.

## You need to rebrand if

- You **distribute a modified version** — a fork with changes must use its
  own name and icon, and must not use the Unpeel name, logo, or
  confusingly-similar marks in its name, domain, or app-store listing. A
  truthful "based on Unpeel" credit is welcome.
- You operate a service or sell a product under the Unpeel name.

## Never

- Imply endorsement by or affiliation with UX Themes AS without written
  permission.
- Use the marks in a way that suggests your builds are official builds:
  official binaries come only from unpeel.com and the official app stores,
  and are the only builds our update and Link infrastructure serves.

## Official builds

The Mac and iOS app sources live in this repository (`apps/`), but official
signed builds — the notarized Mac app served from unpeel.com and its Sparkle
feed, and the iOS app on TestFlight and the App Store — are published only by
the Unpeel team at UX Themes AS with its signing identities. Anyone may build
the apps from source for their own machines. A fork that distributes its own
builds must not use the Unpeel name, app icon, or mascot, must not present
itself as an official build, and must not point at Unpeel's update feeds,
release endpoints, or Link infrastructure.

Questions or permission requests: support@unpeel.com.
